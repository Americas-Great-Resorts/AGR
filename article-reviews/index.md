## About This Collection

This folder holds AI reviews of Americas Great Resorts articles. The three-AI readings below are companions of the pages in the AI Reviews of AGR Articles archive on the AGR website. The single-AI reviews are earlier repository records in which one system reviewed one AGR article. Each record is source-conditioned: the system was given the article and asked for its view. The records are AI readings, not independent validation.

## How These AI Reviews Were Conducted

The same article can produce very different interpretations from Gemini, Google AI, and ChatGPT. Americas Great Resorts asked each system the same question about each article in this collection, using private Firefox windows while logged out. Each review includes the original article link, exact prompt, capture details, and the systems’ responses or labeled excerpts. AGR’s commentary examines what they understood, where their interpretations diverged, and what they added beyond the source.
