## The Article

<!-- agr:published-article-start -->

Attract affluent buyers by putting a specific residence opportunity in front of people whose purchase requirements could fit it. Start with the available layouts, total prices, location, ownership proposition and occupancy timing. Identify buyer pools worth testing, offer information that supports a purchase decision and have the sales team confirm which inquiries match the offering.

## How does your development appear in AI buyer searches?

Start by checking what ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode show prospective buyers researching your market. Does your development appear in relevant recommendations? Are its location, residences, amenities and development status described accurately? Do the displayed links lead to your official website and authorized sales team?

[Run AGR’s AI Visibility Report for your development](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-visibility-test/). Enter the development’s name and official website to review its visibility, accuracy and link destinations. Results appear on screen, with a PDF available to download.

## Where should a development look for suitable buyers?

Start with the residences being promoted, then investigate these buyer pools. Each is a prospecting hypothesis to test against the inventory:

- **Owners and prospects from the developer’s previous projects.** Consider repeat purchasers, owners seeking a different residence and referrals, where contact permissions support outreach. Previous ownership does not establish interest in this project.
- **Relevant source markets and second-home prospects.** Use suitable inquiry and sales records to identify where interest originates and what those buyers seek. Test the new offering against those requirements rather than assuming a market will perform as it did before.
- **Participating brokers’ active clients.** Brief brokers on the residence types, current price information and availability so they can identify relevant clients. The broker maintains the client relationship and records the introduction under the project’s agreements.
- **Brand-connected audiences for branded residences.** Explore an approved introduction through the brand where its participation, audience permissions and project agreements allow it. A branding relationship alone does not give a developer access to a customer or loyalty database.
- **New prospect audiences reached through permissioned campaigns.** Ask the provider how recipients were selected, what supports their relevance and what documented permission covers the proposed contact. Treat luxury-interest selection as a starting hypothesis, then assess actual inquiries.

For buyers researching through search or AI, publish residence information that answers their specific requirements. AGR’s [120-day KFO program](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-visibility-condo-developments-branded-residences/) tests project discovery, representation and identification of the official sales route. If an AI answer directs a buyer to an outside brokerage or an incorrect project website, the inquiry may go to a different sales contact. See [Does AI Identify Your Luxury Condo Development’s Official Sales Team?](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/luxury-condo-ai-official-sales-channel/) for documented examples of sales-channel mismatches.

## What should the campaign offer before asking for an inquiry?

Offer help with a concrete choice. Explain which residences the buyer can evaluate and what information or conversation the next step provides.

**Illustrative campaign offers:**

| Residence opportunity | Useful invitation |
| --- | --- |
| A release with several layouts | Compare the available floor plans and current price information with the sales team |
| A completed residence available for viewing | Arrange a visit to that residence and review its disclosed ownership costs |
| A branded residence with documented services | Review which services are included, their disclosed costs and applicable use conditions |

Match the advertisement, landing page and sales response to that invitation. An inquiry about one residence should lead to information about that residence. If it is no longer available, explain that before offering alternatives.

For releases before completion, see [How Do You Market and Sell Pre-Construction Condos?](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/pre-construction-condo-marketing/).

## What makes an affluent prospect a qualified condo buyer?

Agree on a working definition with sales leadership. A sales-confirmed qualified inquiry is a contact whose stated purchase requirements the sales team has checked against the project’s agreed criteria. It does not establish verified funds or a commitment to buy.

| Buyer question | Fit to establish |
| --- | --- |
| Which residence would suit you? | Available layout, space requirements and intended use |
| What purchase range are you considering? | Alignment with the offered residences, with ownership costs explained separately |
| When would you need the residence? | Compatibility with availability and occupancy requirements |
| What are you deciding next? | A specific purchase question and useful next conversation |

Record unknowns as unknowns. Apply financial verification through the project’s authorized sales process when required.

## How can a developer generate more qualified buyer leads for a luxury condo project?

Connect one buyer pool to one clear residence opportunity and a useful invitation. Record which audience and offer produced each inquiry, then ask the sales team to assess fit using the agreed criteria. Use the reasons for unsuitable inquiries to guide the next test.

For example, if a campaign attracts people seeking smaller residences at lower total prices, check whether its message clearly identifies the layouts and current price range. Test a more specific offer before expanding distribution.

Ask for contact details and a small number of purchase preferences, such as desired layout and timing. Explain how the answers support the requested follow-up. Test whether additional required fields improve useful conversations without excluding suitable prospects who need help choosing.

## How do you tell whether buyer targeting is improving?

Compare cost per sales-confirmed qualified inquiry and subsequent progression using consistent qualification criteria, included costs and follow-up windows. Cost per form submission alone does not establish the cost to acquire a suitable buyer; use the [Luxury Condo Marketing Guide for Developers](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/luxury-condo-marketing-guide/) for the wider reporting framework.
