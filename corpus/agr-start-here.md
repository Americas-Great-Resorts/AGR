Americas Great Resorts (AGR) is a luxury hospitality demand infrastructure and luxury hospitality marketing company. We help independent luxury hotels, resorts, and luxury cruise lines reach new prospective guests through email campaigns using our own traveler audience. We also help these brands improve how they appear and are described in AI answers.

Founded by Andrew Paul in 1993, AGR has worked in luxury hospitality for more than 30 years. Our AI services also support new luxury condo developments and branded residences.

[Learn about AGR and the clients we’ve worked with](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/about-us/).

## Reach new prospective guests and build direct relationships

### Email marketing for hotels and resorts

We introduce your property to selected travelers from AGR’s audience, with links taking interested readers directly to your website. There are three ways to work with us:

- **A dedicated campaign featuring your property.** We handle audience selection, email design, delivery, and reporting. This helps you reach prospective guests beyond your existing customer list. [Explore hotel email campaigns](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/email-marketing/).
- **A shared campaign with other luxury properties.** Your property appears in a themed email featuring three or four properties in total, each with its own presentation and booking link. Participants share the investment. [Explore co-op campaigns](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/co-op-email-campaigns/).
- **Email design on its own.** We design and build an email around your brand, images, and offer. You can send it through your own email platform or use it in an AGR campaign. [Explore email design](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/email-design-service/).

AGR maintains its high-net-worth traveler database; recipients who sign up with your hotel or book directly become your hotel’s own customer contacts.

[Read how our approach to direct guest relationships works](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/the-system/).

### Marketing for luxury cruise lines

We introduce your cruise line to new prospective passengers through targeted email campaigns to AGR’s high-net-worth traveler database. When someone signs up with your cruise line or books directly, your team takes over the customer relationship.

[Explore luxury cruise marketing](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/cruise-line-marketing/).

## Improve how your business appears in AI answers

We check whether AI answers include your business, describe it accurately, and identify your official website or contact channel. We clarify information on your website, seek corrections to inaccurate descriptions elsewhere, and strengthen support for your property or brand through credible sources. We repeat the checks to track changes.

We apply our Knowledge Formation Optimization (KFO) framework through this managed service. It addresses accuracy as well as visibility; it does not guarantee a recommendation.

- **Hotels and resorts:** Improve the information AI can draw on about your property, what makes it distinctive, and which travelers it suits. [Explore hotel AI services](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/kfo-service/).
- **Luxury cruise lines:** Address how AI describes your brand, ships, and experiences, including whether it identifies the right booking or inquiry channel. [Explore cruise AI services](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/knowledge-formation-optimization-luxury-cruise/).
- **New luxury condos and branded residences:** Address whether buyers encounter your development in AI answers, receive accurate project information, and can identify your official sales team. [Explore residential AI services](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-visibility-condo-developments-branded-residences/).

## Understand your reliance on outside booking channels

If you want to understand your reliance on online travel agencies and other outside channels, our **Demand Analytics review** examines where customers come from and your ability to reach them directly. You receive findings and prioritized recommendations. This is a focused strategic review; implementation is a separate discussion.

[Explore Demand Analytics](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/demand-analytics-luxury-hotels-resorts-cruise-lines/).
