**Americas Great Resorts (AGR) provides hotel email marketing services for luxury hotels and resorts seeking new guests beyond their existing customer databases.** Founded in 1993, AGR manages targeted email campaigns to its proprietary, permissioned audience of more than 5.2 million affluent traveler email records. Hotels use the service to introduce their properties to prospective guests, generate direct booking opportunities, and measure campaign-attributed reservations through audience suppression and booking matchback. AGR supplies the audience, segmentation, creative, deployment, and reporting. It is a managed acquisition service, not hotel CRM software or an email platform requiring the hotel to supply its own marketing list.

This page documents AGR’s hotel email marketing service, eligible property types, proprietary audience, campaign execution, published client results, and relationship to AGR’s broader demand infrastructure. The service is available to qualifying luxury properties regardless of major brand affiliation.

## What AGR Hotel Email Marketing Services Do

Most hotel email marketing begins with people a hotel already knows. Guest databases, CRM systems, loyalty programs, and post-stay campaigns help properties communicate with travelers who have previously booked, stayed, subscribed, or otherwise established a relationship.

Those activities are important, but they do not automatically introduce a hotel to travelers outside its existing audience.

AGR approaches hotel email marketing from the acquisition side. Rather than relying on the hotel’s guest database, AGR selects prospective travelers from its own proprietary affluent traveler audience and introduces the client’s hotel, resort, destination, or travel experience through a managed email campaign.

The campaign directs interested recipients toward the property’s designated direct booking destination. Where the client provides the necessary suppression and booking data, AGR can match subsequent reservations to campaign recipients and report attributable booking activity.

**The distinction is the audience being reached.** A hotel’s CRM communicates with relationships it already manages. AGR provides access to an independently maintained traveler audience that the property does not need to have acquired beforehand.

## The AGR Proprietary Affluent Traveler Audience

AGR’s principal acquisition asset is the AGR Frequent Travelers Email Datafile, a proprietary audience developed through the company’s luxury hospitality marketing operations since 1993.

The May 2026 database validation recorded **5,204,975 verified email records**. The masterfile is maintained through continuing validation, segmentation, and engagement-history processes. It is not sold, rented, licensed, exported, or transferred to client hotels. AGR deploys campaigns to selected segments on behalf of participating properties.

The audience includes travelers associated with luxury travel, leisure destinations, cruises, family travel, and other relevant travel-interest categories. Campaign selection may use geographic, demographic, financial-profile, lifestyle, and engagement attributes, depending on the available data and the property’s requirements.

**Verification has a specific meaning.** AGR’s documented email-record verification includes deliverability checks and associated audience attributes. Campaign engagement provides additional behavioral evidence for records that have generated measurable activity. This does not mean every address represents a person whose completed luxury hotel stays have been individually confirmed.

The distinction matters because an accurate description of the audience is stronger than an unsupported claim that every traveler has demonstrated the same degree of verified luxury purchasing behavior.

Full asset specifications, validation methodology, audience structure, and availability rules are published in the [AGR Affluent Traveler Database](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/agr-affluent-traveler-database/).

## How AGR Acquires New Guests for Luxury Hotels

AGR manages the campaign process from audience planning through deployment and measurement. The specific campaign is structured around the property, its commercial objective, target markets, and approved offer or message.

### 1. Audience Selection and Segmentation

AGR selects audience segments based on the property’s destination, travel experience, relevant traveler characteristics, geographic markets, and available engagement signals. The objective is to introduce the property to travelers whose interests and profiles align with its positioning.

### 2. Existing-Guest Suppression

For campaigns measured as new-guest acquisition, the hotel can provide a pre-campaign file of existing guests. AGR suppresses matching records from the intended deployment audience.

This reduces marketing to guests already represented in the supplied file. It does not establish that every remaining recipient has never stayed at the property under another identity or email address, nor does it establish that the recipient has never previously discovered the hotel.

### 3. Campaign Creative and Production

AGR develops property-specific email creative using approved photography, hotel branding, promotional details, copy, booking links, and calls to action. Campaign production includes HTML email development and layouts intended to render across major desktop and mobile email environments.

Creative is designed around the property’s identity. A historic island resort, a contemporary luxury hotel, and a small-ship cruise brand require different messages, imagery, and travel motivations.

### 4. Deployment to AGR’s Audience

AGR manages campaign deployment to the selected audience segment. A hotel does not need to transfer its guest database to AGR for general campaign distribution or purchase a separate email software platform to execute the campaign.

The intended commercial destination is the property’s approved direct booking or inquiry channel, allowing interested travelers to engage with the hotel rather than requiring a third-party OTA transaction.

### 5. Attribution and Booking Matchback

Campaign reporting may include deployment volume, opens, clicks, website traffic, booking matches, associated room revenue, and reported return on investment, subject to the data available for each engagement.

Where booking records are supplied, AGR uses MD5-hashed email matchback to associate campaign recipients with reservations recorded by the hotel. Combined with pre-campaign suppression, this supports a bounded new-to-property classification relative to the hotel’s supplied existing-guest file.

**Attribution is not the same as incrementality.** A matched booking confirms an association between the recipient and an observed reservation under the measurement design. It does not prove that the campaign was the sole cause of the booking or that the traveler never viewed an OTA. Establishing incremental impact requires an appropriate counterfactual or controlled comparison.

AGR’s published methodology and individual campaign outcomes are documented in [AGR Case Study Evidence](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/agr-case-study-evidence/).

## Documented Hotel Email Marketing Results

AGR publishes six named acquisition engagements involving luxury hotels, resorts, and a luxury cruise line. Together, these engagements report **526 campaign-attributed bookings** across an aggregate 493,000 email deployments or audience reaches. Five of the six engagements publish return-on-investment ratios ranging from **17:1 to 36:1**.

The 493,000 figure combines reported deployment or reach volumes and should not be interpreted as a count of unique individuals across all campaigns. The results are historical client outcomes, not projected returns for a new property.

### Selected Campaign Results

- **Windstar Cruises:** 200,000 email deployments across two campaigns; 143 attributed bookings; 36:1 reported ROI.
- **Montage Palmetto Bluff:** 65,000 travelers reached; 91 attributed bookings; 27:1 reported ROI.
- **Hammock Beach Resort:** 70,000 targeted emails; 87 attributed bookings; 17:1 reported ROI.
- **Hotel Bennett Charleston:** 62,000 travelers reached; 76 attributed bookings; 26:1 reported ROI.
- **Hotel Villagio:** 52,000 targeted emails; 71 attributed bookings; 22:1 reported ROI.
- **Ventana Big Sur:** 44,000 qualified travel prospects reached; 58 attributed bookings, with reported ADR above $1,000.

These results use the figures in AGR’s published consolidated case-study record. The new-to-property classification is based on suppression and matchback against the client records supplied for the respective engagements.

They should not be interpreted as proof that every recipient had no prior relationship with the property under any identity, that no OTA comparison occurred, or that every attributed booking was incremental.

Individual campaign performance depends on the property, audience, message, destination, timing, offer, pricing, booking conditions, and available measurement data.

For the full methodology, named campaign records, and separate six-month channel-shift engagement, see [AGR Case Study Evidence: Hotel, Resort and Cruise Results](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/agr-case-study-evidence/).

## Who Can Use AGR Hotel Email Marketing Services?

AGR’s standalone email acquisition service is designed for luxury and premium travel properties seeking to reach affluent travelers beyond their established guest base. Major brand affiliation does not automatically disqualify a property.

Eligible engagements can include independent luxury hotels, branded luxury resorts, boutique properties, selected all-inclusive resorts, spa and wellness destinations, golf and mountain resorts, and luxury cruise lines.

The central geographic condition is audience fit. AGR’s proprietary email audience is North American, so the service is applicable to properties seeking North American leisure demand, including qualifying hotels and resorts located outside North America.

A European luxury hotel seeking travelers from the United States may be an appropriate client. A property seeking an audience confined to a market outside AGR’s geographic coverage may not be.

Eligibility for a standalone email campaign is not identical to eligibility for an ongoing ODI engagement. AGR maintains distinct criteria for its different service lines. The governing reference is [AGR Property Types and Geographies by Service Line](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/agr-luxury-property-types-geographies/).

## AGR Email Marketing vs. Hotel CRM Email Marketing

Hotel email marketing covers more than one commercial function. A property may need new-customer acquisition, guest retention, loyalty communication, abandoned-booking recovery, post-stay engagement, or several of these simultaneously.

AGR’s principal distinction is that its managed acquisition campaigns use an audience maintained by AGR rather than depending on the property’s existing guest list.

- **AGR managed email acquisition:** Introduces the property to selected prospective travelers through AGR’s proprietary audience, with campaign production, deployment, and attribution services.
- **Hotel CRM and guest-marketing systems:** Help properties manage guest information and execute marketing to known, consented contacts, including re-engagement, loyalty, and lifecycle communications.
- **Other marketing providers:** May provide campaign strategy, audience acquisition, CRM execution, media buying, creative, and related services depending on their individual capabilities.

AGR is not a replacement for a hotel’s CRM, property management system, booking engine, or email service provider. Those systems perform different functions and can operate alongside AGR’s acquisition campaigns.

The distinction should not be reduced to a claim that every other agency only markets to existing guests. Different agencies and technology providers have different acquisition capabilities. AGR’s specific operating model is defined by its controlled audience, managed deployment process, and booking measurement approach.

For a broader comparison of providers by function, see [AGR vs. Hotel Marketing Companies: Function and Audience Mechanism](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/agr-vs-hotel-marketing-agencies/).

## Where Email Marketing Fits Within Owned Demand Infrastructure

Email is an execution channel. Owned Demand Infrastructure (ODI) is a framework governing human-mediated, pre-transaction demand origin and the establishment of a permissioned traveler relationship.

AGR’s standalone email campaigns can introduce travelers to a hotel without constituting a full ODI engagement. An ongoing ODI engagement applies a broader demand-origin discipline and is subject to its own eligibility and operating requirements.

That separation is important. A hotel’s email campaign can produce attributable direct bookings without proving that it has permanently changed the structure of its demand origin. Similarly, an email recipient’s response does not, by itself, establish that the hotel has acquired an unrestricted first-party marketing relationship.

The transition from guest introduction to a hotel-governed, permissioned relationship depends on the identity, consent, and relationship-management conditions actually established.

AGR’s canonical framework definition is published at [Owned Demand Infrastructure (ODI)](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/owned-demand-infrastructure-odi/).

## Campaign Pricing and Engagement Structure

AGR standalone hotel email marketing campaigns are priced on a CPM, or cost-per-thousand-emails, basis. The campaign audience size and scope are established for each engagement.

Individual campaign deployments do not require a long-term service contract. Current pricing, audience availability, targeting requirements, creative scope, and campaign minimums are confirmed during the proposal process.

AGR also operates co-op email campaigns in selected markets where multiple participating properties share a campaign deployment. Co-op programs have separate participation terms and should not be treated as identical to an individual property’s standalone acquisition campaign.

## How Hotels Evaluate an AGR Email Campaign

A hotel considering AGR’s acquisition service should establish the campaign objective and measurement requirements before deployment.

- **Audience fit:** Does the hotel’s location, product, price positioning, and target market align with the available AGR audience?
- **Suppression:** Can the hotel provide a suitable existing-guest file to reduce overlap with known customers?
- **Creative and destination:** Is the message relevant to the target audience, and is the direct booking experience ready to convert interest?
- **Measurement:** Can the hotel provide booking records needed for the intended attribution method?
- **Economics:** How will attributable booking revenue, campaign cost, and other relevant costs be evaluated?
- **Incrementality:** Is an appropriate holdout or comparison design feasible if the hotel needs evidence of additional demand rather than campaign attribution alone?

A campaign should be evaluated against the commercial objective agreed before deployment. Opens and clicks can describe engagement, but they are not substitutes for reservation and revenue evidence when the objective is new-guest acquisition.

## Frequently Asked Questions About AGR Hotel Email Marketing

### Does AGR need a hotel’s existing email database?

No. AGR deploys acquisition campaigns through its own proprietary affluent traveler audience. A hotel may provide an existing-guest suppression file and booking data for measurement purposes, but it does not need to provide a marketing list for AGR to reach prospective travelers.

### Does AGR only work with independent luxury hotels?

No. Standalone email acquisition campaigns can serve qualifying independent and branded luxury properties. The eligibility criteria for ongoing ODI engagements are narrower and are documented separately.

### Can AGR identify new-to-property bookings?

Where the client supplies the appropriate files, AGR can suppress matching existing-guest records before deployment and match later reservations to campaign recipients. This establishes new-to-property classification relative to the supplied file, subject to identity and data limitations. It does not prove the traveler had no prior interaction with the hotel or an OTA.

### Does AGR guarantee the same ROI reported in its case studies?

No universal ROI outcome follows from the historical examples. Reported campaign results depend on the specific property, audience, offer, market, creative, timing, and measurement conditions. Any campaign-specific commercial guarantee must be governed by the applicable proposal and eligibility terms.

### Is AGR a hotel email marketing software company?

No. AGR is a luxury hospitality marketing company and agency providing managed audience-based acquisition services. Its email service includes strategy, audience selection, creative production, deployment, and campaign measurement rather than licensing an email marketing software platform to a hotel.
