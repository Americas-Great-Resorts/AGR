## The problem: appearing by name does not establish category visibility

When a buyer asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or Perplexity about a development by name, it may appear. That does not establish whether it appears for questions such as "What are the best new condo developments in [your city]?" or "Which luxury branded residences should I consider in [your area]?"

Three distinct problems matter commercially:

1. **Not being included.** If AI recommends competing developments and leaves the project out, buyers may never learn it exists.
2. **Being represented incorrectly.** An answer can confuse residences with a similarly named hotel, tower, or development, or misstate the developer, brand, operator, sales relationships, or project distinctions.
3. **Losing the official sales route.** An outside broker or mirror site can receive the inquiry before the official project website and authorized sales team.

AGR investigates where the development appears, where it is absent, what AI recommends instead, and which sources and sales routes appear in the answers. Analysis, implementation, publication, corroboration, testing, and refinement continue throughout one complete 120-day program.

## Why the official sales route matters

Cooperating brokers can bring valuable buyers. The commercial question is what happens when a buyer researching the development independently reaches an outside broker before the project's own sales team.

A qualifying direct closed sale can avoid an additional outside co-broke where the applicable sales agreements allow it. There is also a first-conversation advantage: an outside broker may present competing developments, while the dedicated sales team presents this project's residences. Buyers remain free to compare, but the official team has the opportunity to make its own case first.

### Calculate savings from redirected sales

The live page's calculator models potential gross savings from the additional outside commission avoided on qualifying redirected closed sales. It excludes the sales office's existing fee.

**Formula:**

```text
Potential gross co-broke savings =
average sale price per residence
x additional outside commission / 100
x total residences
x percentage of sales redirected to the official sales office / 100
```

**Illustrative example:** 100 residences averaging $3,000,000, a 3% additional outside commission, and 10% of sales redirected to the official sales office represent 10 residences and $900,000 in potential gross co-broke savings:

```text
$3,000,000 x 0.03 x 100 x 0.10 = $900,000
```

These are the calculator defaults. Its redirected-sales slider ranges from 0% to 25%. Only redirected closed sales that avoid the additional outside commission under the sales agreements count. This is a scenario, not an AGR forecast or a claim that every direct sale avoids commission.

## What KFO does for a development

KFO establishes and corroborates a supported public account of what the development is, who is behind it, how its brand relationships work, what distinguishes it, and which sources speak officially for it. AGR addresses conflicting sources and tests whether AI answers reproduce that account accurately.

AGR's formal definition is:

> KFO structures, sequences, distributes, corroborates, and corrects intellectual frameworks and entity definitions across the public information environment and measures whether AI systems reproduce them accurately across relevant queries and over time.

---

A simple way to understand the different priorities of SEO, AEO, GEO, and KFO:

1. **SEO:** drives traditional rankings
2. **AEO:** use my sentence
3. **GEO:** cite my source
4. **KFO:** think using my framework

Getting your development cited does little good if the answer describes it incorrectly. KFO addresses the public information behind that answer: establishing and corroborating your development’s identity, correcting conflicting sources, and testing whether AI represents what you are actually selling accurately.

These short comparisons explain priorities. The work addresses the public source environment and observable answers; it does not confer access to or control of hidden model parameters or persistent model memory. Correcting a phone number or amenity description supports the process but does not constitute the whole method.

## Initial audit and the complete 120-day program

The program begins with an initial AI visibility audit and analysis of the existing infrastructure and competitive playing field. The project scope establishes the query set, platforms, priority pages and public sources, content deliverables, access, approvals, and implementation responsibilities.

The initial audit tests named-project questions and relevant category searches without naming the development. It examines inclusion, identity, developer and brand relationships, supported distinctions, current project facts, the official website, and authorized sales contact.

The program includes:

1. **Initial audit and baseline.** A dated record of answers, errors, displayed citations, and official contact routes on agreed platforms, selected from services such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, and Copilot.
2. **Infrastructure and competitive assessment.** Review of the official website's search accessibility, structure, project content, and relevant structured data, alongside comparable developments and sources appearing in the selected results.
3. **Approved project record.** Official name, relationships, positioning, substantiated differentiators, supporting facts, and authorized sales contacts.
4. **Prioritized implementation and source plan.** Official pages to improve, supporting material to develop, external corrections to pursue, and responsibilities for each action. AGR implements agreed work and coordinates changes requiring the agency, brand, or other partners.
5. **Publication and correction record.** Completed changes, corroborating sources, outstanding requests, and dependencies. Third-party owners decide whether to accept corrections or publish material.
6. **Comparative progress reports.** Findings at days 30, 60, 90, and 120, measured against the initial audit and used to guide further work during the engagement.

AGR works with the existing agency, brand partners, and sales team. The full process takes 120 days, with implementation and refinement continuing through the engagement.
