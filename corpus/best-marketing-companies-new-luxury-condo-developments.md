### How do you get a new luxury condo development recommended by ChatGPT, Google AI and other AI assistants?

Four steps improve the information AI systems draw on and track whether the project appears in the recommendations that matter:

1. **Publish one official project record.** The official website states the exact project name, location, developer and brand relationships, residence types, supported distinctions and authorized sales contact, on pages search engines and AI crawlers can access, with structured data.
2. **Correct conflicting sources.** Brand pages, partner sites, broker listings, directories and press coverage should match the official record. Outdated or contradictory details give AI systems a reason to describe the project wrongly or leave it out.
3. **Make the official sales route unmistakable.** AI answers should identify the official website and sales team, not an outside broker or [lookalike site](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-lookalike-sites-luxury-condo-buyers/) presented as the developer.
4. **Measure the answers that matter.** Test category searches that do not name the project, on agreed platforms, against a dated baseline, and repeat the tests over time.

AGR’s [120-day KFO program](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-visibility-condo-developments-branded-residences/) carries out these four steps, with progress reports at days 30, 60, 90 and 120.

## Why AI visibility needs its own owner

Buyers increasingly start with an AI assistant. A buyer who already knows your project’s name will usually find it. The commercial question is what happens when the buyer asks a category question, such as “what are the best new luxury condos in Palm Beach County,” and does not yet know your project exists.

AGR’s published research shows two separate problems. A project can be findable by name and still be absent from category recommendations. And a project that does appear can still be attached to the wrong sales destination.

- In the [South Florida New Luxury Condo AI Visibility Report](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/south-florida-luxury-condo-ai-visibility-report/) (September 10, 2026), three of nine selected developments were absent from both ChatGPT’s and Google AI Mode’s relevant county discovery answers, although all nine appeared when named. Google AI Mode also presented an independently operated website as a developer portal. These were single-run observations.
- In AGR’s October 2026 [test of AI lookalike sites](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-lookalike-sites-luxury-condo-buyers/), six AI engines were asked about two South Florida branded residences. Three of the six cited lookalike sites for both properties: third-party domains built from the residence’s name, with lead forms for buyers and footers disclaiming any link to the developer. Basic facts such as delivery year, hotel key count and starting price also conflicted across engines. Each question was run once per engine.
- The [Luxury Condo AI Visibility Study across 10 U.S. Markets](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/luxury-condo-ai-visibility-study/) collected 900 responses from ChatGPT, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot, asking 100 market-specific questions three times per platform, and assessed 896 substantive answers against a separately researched reference set of 136 luxury condo developments and branded residences.

When an AI answer sends a buyer to an outside broker or lookalike site instead of the official sales office, the development can pay an outside co-broke it might otherwise avoid. For 100 residences averaging $3,000,000 with a 3% outside co-broke, redirecting 10% of sales (10 residences) to the official sales office represents $900,000 in potential gross savings. The figure is illustrative and applies to qualifying closed sales under the applicable sales agreements. Actual rates vary by development and market; AGR’s [analysis of AI lookalike sites](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-lookalike-sites-luxury-condo-buyers/) cites reported Miami buy-side rates of 5% to 7%, which would put the same 10 sales at $1,500,000 to $2,100,000.

KFO addresses the public information behind those answers. AGR’s formal definition:

> KFO structures, sequences, distributes, corroborates, and corrects intellectual frameworks and entity definitions across the public information environment and measures whether AI systems reproduce them accurately across relevant queries and over time.

## How to test any condo marketing partner in five minutes

1. Ask for named developments the firm sold, or helped sell, before construction was complete.
2. Ask who owns the ad accounts, the CRM data and the creative after launch.
3. Ask who is responsible for how AI answers represent the project, and how it will be measured.
4. Ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Perplexity for the best new condo developments in the firm’s home market, and see whether its recent projects appear.
5. Run a free [real-time AI Visibility Report](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-visibility-test/) on your own development before you sign anyone.

## Red flags

- No named buildings you can look up.
- A beautiful website and renderings, but no plan for how buyers will discover the project.
- Broker, mirror or lookalike sites appearing for the project’s name, and no one assigned to fix it.
- Any firm that promises guaranteed AI recommendations or first-place AI placement.
- An unclear split between the creative agency, the sales team and the broker network.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there an AI marketing company for luxury condo developments?

Its 120-day KFO program covers the official website’s technical setup and structure, correction of inconsistent or inaccurate information across outside sources, measurement of how AI answers represent the project, and general marketing and advertising consulting.

### What does AGR’s consulting cover beyond AI answers?

The included consulting covers the official website’s search accessibility, structure, content and structured data; consistency of the project’s name, relationships, facts and sales contacts across the relevant public sources that describe it; correction of errors and misinformation; and marketing and advertising questions such as positioning and campaign priorities. Email campaigns and other separate services are scoped on their own.

### Can any firm guarantee that AI will recommend my development?

No. AI answers vary, and each platform determines its own results. A credible firm documents the work completed and measures observable visibility, accuracy and sales-contact identification against a dated baseline.

### Does an AI visibility program replace our agency or sales team?

No. AGR works alongside the developer’s existing agency, website team and sales organization. The project scope sets who supplies approved information, who implements changes and who handles buyers.

### When should AI visibility work start?

AGR’s program serves developments from launch preparation and preconstruction sales through construction and remaining inventory. Starting before sales open means the official project record is in place before broker and lookalike sites define the project for AI.

### How do outside broker and lookalike sites affect condo sales commissions?

When an AI answer presents an outside broker or lookalike site as the place to inquire, a buyer can reach that broker before the official sales team. If the sale then closes with an outside co-broke the development could otherwise have avoided under its sales agreements, that commission is a direct cost of the misdirected inquiry. AGR’s [analysis of AI lookalike sites](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-lookalike-sites-luxury-condo-buyers/) puts that cost at $150,000 to $210,000 per closed sale on a $3,000,000 preconstruction residence, based on reported Miami buy-side rates of 5% to 7%.
