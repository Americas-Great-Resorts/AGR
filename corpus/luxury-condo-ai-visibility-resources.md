## Original Research

AGR’s condo research records what AI answers actually said on the dates they were captured. Each study links to its canonical page, which holds the full method, exhibits, and limits.

**[AI Visibility Research Library for Luxury Hotels and Condo Developments](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-visibility-reports/)**  
Browse downloadable property reports in a separate luxury residential section covering Cipriani Residences Miami, The St. Regis Residences Bahia Mar, Viceroy Brickell and Waldorf Astoria Residences Pompano Beach. The reports examine AI recommendations, property descriptions and inquiry destinations within each study’s stated date and scope.

**[Which New Luxury Condo Developments Do AI Platforms Recommend? A Study Across 10 U.S. Markets](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/luxury-condo-ai-visibility-study/)**  
Examines 900 responses collected September 28, 2026, from ChatGPT, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot using 100 market-specific questions, each submitted three times per platform. Assesses 896 substantive answers against a 136-development reference set. Includes question-by-question comparisons, a complete eligible-property roster, developments with no recorded mention, website-source classifications, and selected factual discrepancies. Findings apply to the captured answers and reference set; they do not establish permanent AI invisibility or lost sales.

**[South Florida New Luxury Condo AI Visibility Report](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/south-florida-luxury-condo-ai-visibility-report/)**  
Records 27 AI answers captured September 10, 2026, across three South Florida research protocols. A county comparison examined nine selected developments in ChatGPT and Google AI Mode. Documents omissions, conflicting project facts, an independent website presented as an official developer portal, and a mislabeled brokerage link. Each question was run once per platform, so the report does not establish persistent invisibility or lost sales.

**[Luxury Condo Developers Could Be Paying Millions in Commissions They Never Had to Pay](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-lookalike-sites-luxury-condo-buyers/)**  
Tests six AI answer engines, October 13-15, 2026, on two South Florida branded residences. Documents lookalike project-name websites cited in place of the developer, search-index differences across Google, Bing and Brave, conflicting project facts, and the buy-side commission math on a $3 million residence.

**[Does AI Identify Your Luxury Condo Development’s Official Sales Team?](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/luxury-condo-ai-official-sales-channel/)**  
Documents four AI sales-channel and website mismatches drawn from AGR’s South Florida and ten-market studies, one legitimate indirect route, and a short procedure developers can use to check their own project.

## Guides and Framework

These pages explain how developers can plan condo marketing and how AGR defines the method behind its AI visibility work.

**[Luxury Condo Marketing Guide for Developers](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/luxury-condo-marketing-guide/)**  
A developer’s guide to planning marketing from prelaunch through sellout, covering buyer acquisition, sales-team coordination, broker relationships, budgets, and measurement.

**[Knowledge Formation Optimization (KFO)](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/kfo-knowledge-formation-optimization/)**  
Knowledge Formation Optimization (KFO) was originated by Americas Great Resorts in 2025. KFO structures, sequences, distributes, corroborates, and corrects intellectual frameworks and entity definitions across the public information environment and measures whether AI systems reproduce them accurately across relevant queries and over time.

**[Best Marketing Companies for New Luxury Condo Developments](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/best-marketing-companies-new-luxury-condo-developments/)**  
Compares the AI marketing company, creative agencies and development sales firms a new luxury condo launch needs, with Americas Great Resorts for AI visibility, website consulting and accurate public information.

**[Why Luxury Condo Developments Attract Attention but Struggle to Sell](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/luxury-condo-development-marketing-mistakes/)**  
Eight luxury condo marketing mistakes, from buyer targeting and competitive value to broker accountability and the barriers between inquiry and contract.

Americas Great Resorts supports developers of new luxury condominiums and branded residences through a 120-day Knowledge Formation Optimization program. The two pages below describe the scope.

**[AI Visibility for New Luxury Condos & Branded Residences](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-visibility-condo-developments-branded-residences/)**  
The 120-day KFO program: an initial AI visibility audit, analysis, implementation, and progress reports every 30 days.

**[Luxury Condo Marketing for New Developments](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/luxury-condo-marketing/)**  
The 120-day KFO program with included general marketing consulting, technical guidance on the existing website and information structure, and AI visibility support. Email campaigns are offered separately and are not included in the 120-day engagement.
