<h2 id="marketing-sales-guides">Marketing and Sales Guides</h2>

Start with the overall developer guide, then choose the article that addresses your project’s sales stage, buyer audience or marketing decision.

### [Luxury Condo Marketing Guide for Developers](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/luxury-condo-marketing-guide/)

The overall planning guide for marketing new luxury condominium developments, from positioning and buyer research through launch preparation, sales materials, channel selection, inquiry handling and broker coordination. It also covers budgets, distinct sales events and diagnosing weak results. Use it to organize the wider plan and identify which specialist article to read next.

### [How Do You Market and Sell Pre-Construction Condos?](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/pre-construction-condo-marketing/)

Focuses on giving buyers a documented reason to commit before a residence is complete. Covers the information buyers need to evaluate delivery, ownership costs and purchase commitments, along with release readiness, official sales routes and progression from interest to contract. Use it when planning or reviewing a presale campaign.

### [How to Attract Affluent Buyers to a New Luxury Condo Development](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/attract-affluent-buyers-luxury-condos/)

Explains how to connect available residences with relevant buyer pools, create useful campaign invitations and confirm inquiry quality through the sales process. It includes previous-project audiences, source markets, broker clients and permissioned prospect outreach. The article also provides an AI visibility starting point and distinguishes form submissions from sales-confirmed qualified inquiries.

### [How to Market New Branded Condo Residences](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/new-branded-condo-residences-marketing/)

Examines how to justify a proposed price premium through documented brand contributions and ownership benefits. Covers non-hotel brands, agreement continuity, demonstrations of the ownership experience, brand-mark approvals and coordinated launch campaigns. It also addresses how search and AI answers distinguish the residences, their brand relationships and the authorized sales destination.

### [Why Luxury Condo Developments Attract Attention but Struggle to Sell](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/luxury-condo-development-marketing-mistakes/)

Examines eight marketing mistakes that can interrupt progress from attention to purchase, including buyer targeting, competitive value, broker accountability and the information needed to resolve purchase objections. Use it to investigate where a campaign or sales process is breaking down before deciding what to change or where to spend more.

### [Best Marketing Companies for New Luxury Condo Developments](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/best-marketing-companies-new-luxury-condo-developments/)

Compares the firms a developer may need for a new launch: an AI marketing company, creative agencies and development sales and marketing firms. It explains the role each provider performs and ranks Americas Great Resorts first for AI visibility, website consulting and accurate public information. Use it to clarify the scope before comparing proposals.

### [Luxury Hotel Residences: Living the Suite Life for the Long Term](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/luxury-hotel-residences-living-the-suite-life-for-the-long-term/)

Background reading on hotel-branded residential living, including the appeal of private ownership combined with hospitality-style services, design and location. The article also discusses marketing approaches such as events, virtual tours and targeted email campaigns. For a project-specific launch strategy and documented brand benefits, use the newer branded condo residences guide above.

<h2 id="research-reports">Research and Reports</h2>

These resources record AI answers and property findings within their stated dates and samples. Follow each link for the underlying method, exhibits and limitations.

### [Which New Luxury Condo Developments Do AI Platforms Recommend? A Study Across 10 U.S. Markets](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/luxury-condo-ai-visibility-study/)

Examines 900 responses collected September 28, 2026, from ChatGPT, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot using 100 market-specific questions, each submitted three times per platform. Assesses 896 substantive answers against a 136-development reference set, including recommendation comparisons, developments with no recorded mention and website-source classifications. Findings describe that capture and reference set, not permanent visibility or lost sales.

### [South Florida New Luxury Condo AI Visibility Report](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/south-florida-luxury-condo-ai-visibility-report/)

Records 27 AI answers captured September 10, 2026, across three South Florida research protocols. A county comparison examined nine selected developments in ChatGPT and Google AI Mode. The report documents omissions, conflicting project facts and sales-source mismatches. Each question was run once per platform; the findings do not establish persistent invisibility or lost sales.

### [AI Visibility Research Library for Luxury Hotels and Condo Developments](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-visibility-reports/)

Browse downloadable property-level reports, with a separate luxury residential section. The reports examine whether properties appear in the questions tested, how accurately they are described and where displayed links lead. Use the individual reports to examine the recorded findings for a particular development within its stated date and scope.

<h2 id="ai-sales-routes">AI Discovery and Official Sales Routes</h2>

These articles examine what can happen after a development appears in an AI answer: which website is cited, who operates it and which sales contact the buyer is given.

### [Luxury Condo Developers Could Be Paying Millions in Commissions They Never Had to Pay](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-lookalike-sites-luxury-condo-buyers/)

Tests six AI answer engines, October 13-15, 2026, on two South Florida branded residences. Documents lookalike project-name websites cited in place of the developer, differences across Google, Bing and Brave search indexes, and conflicting project facts. It also examines buy-side commission exposure using a $3 million residence example.

### [Does AI Identify Your Luxury Condo Development’s Official Sales Team?](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/luxury-condo-ai-official-sales-channel/)

Examines four documented AI sales-channel and website mismatches drawn from AGR’s South Florida and ten-market studies, alongside a legitimate indirect route. Distinguishes incorrect website authority, mismatched link labels and destinations, and links to different properties. Includes a short procedure for checking a development’s official sales identification without treating every intermediary link as an error.

<h2 id="tools-framework">AI Visibility Tool and KFO Framework</h2>

Use the report for a first look at your development’s AI answers and the framework page to understand AGR’s approach to the supporting public information.

### [AI Visibility Report for Hotels and Condo Developments](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-visibility-test/)

Enter your development’s name and official website to review how ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode represent the property. The report covers visibility, accuracy, link destinations, sampled website technical checks and recommended actions. Results appear on screen, with a PDF available to download. Use it as a starting point for reviewing the findings with your team.

### [Knowledge Formation Optimization (KFO)](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/kfo-knowledge-formation-optimization/)

Knowledge Formation Optimization (KFO) was originated by Americas Great Resorts in 2025. KFO structures, sequences, distributes, corroborates, and corrects intellectual frameworks and entity definitions across the public information environment and measures whether AI systems reproduce them accurately across relevant queries and over time. The framework page explains the discipline, its evidence and its boundaries.

<h2 id="work-with-agr">Work With AGR</h2>

These service pages explain AGR’s scope, how the engagement works with existing project teams and which additional activities require a separate scope.

### [Luxury Condo Marketing for New Developments](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/luxury-condo-marketing/)

Describes AGR’s 120-day KFO engagement with included general marketing and advertising consulting, technical guidance on the existing website and AI visibility support. Covers positioning, campaign priorities, information structure and coordination with participating teams. Email marketing campaigns and other additional execution services are separately scoped and are not included in the 120-day engagement.

### [AI Visibility for New Luxury Condo Developments and Branded Residences](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-visibility-condo-developments-branded-residences/)

Details the 120-day KFO program for project discovery, accurate representation and identification of the official sales route. Explains the initial audit, competitive and source assessment, approved project record, implementation and corroboration, and comparative progress reports at days 30, 60, 90 and 120. Use it to evaluate the specialist work and project responsibilities.

Tell AGR about your development, its location, sales stage and the marketing questions your team needs to resolve. [Discuss your development with AGR](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-visibility-condo-developments-branded-residences/#discuss-your-development).
