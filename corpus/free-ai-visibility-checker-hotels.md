## What is an AI visibility checker for hotels and condo developments?

An AI visibility checker examines how AI systems answer questions about hotels, new luxury condo developments, branded residences, and destinations. For property teams, the practical questions are whether the property appears in relevant recommendations, how it is described, which competitors appear, and whether links lead to the property’s official website or elsewhere.

Two different questions illustrate why this matters. “Tell me about Hotel X” tests **branded recognition**, where the property is already named. “Which luxury hotels in this destination are best for a quiet couples’ weekend?” tests **unbranded discovery**, where the traveler has not selected a property. Recognition does not establish inclusion in recommendation lists.

## What AGR’s free AI visibility checker examines

AGR’s AI Visibility Report examines answers from **ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Mode**, alongside selected checks of the property’s official website.

- **Local AI recommendations:** Whether the property appears in the recommendation answers tested.
- **Competing properties:** Which other hotels, new condo developments, or branded residences appear in those answers.
- **Factual accuracy:** How claims about location, amenities, services, and other property details compare with official information.
- **Link destinations:** Whether property-related links lead to the official website, a booking platform, a broker, or another source.
- **Website technical checks:** Findings from sampled pages, including indexing restrictions, structured data, page titles, descriptions, and other technical issues.
- **Recommended actions:** Specific corrections and opportunities supported by the report’s findings.

The report is an immediate, point-in-time diagnostic.

## Why a hotel can be known to AI but missing from recommendations

A property team may ask ChatGPT to describe its hotel and receive a detailed, accurate response. That does not answer whether the same hotel appears when travelers search by occasion, amenity, neighborhood, or destination without naming it.

For example, travelers might ask for a luxury beachfront resort, an intimate wedding venue, or a hotel near a particular attraction. Different questions can produce different sets of recommended properties.

The practical value of a recommendation check is identifying where a property appears, where it does not, and which competitors the systems return for the questions tested. Those competitor lists can help reveal how AI systems currently categorize the property and which hotels occupy recommendation opportunities the property might expect to compete for.

## Why AI visibility matters for new luxury condo developments and branded residences

For a new luxury condominium development, the discovery challenge begins before a buyer knows the property’s name. Prospective buyers may ask AI systems about new waterfront condominiums, branded residences, residences with hotel-style amenities, or luxury developments in a particular destination. A project can have a polished website and detailed sales materials yet be missing from the developments named in those answers.

During preconstruction and presales, those omissions deserve attention. The report helps a developer or sales and marketing team examine whether the development appears in relevant recommendations, which competing projects are presented, whether AI descriptions reflect official project information, and whether property-related links lead to the development’s website or elsewhere.

For branded condominium developments, accurate representation also matters. The brand relationship, location, proposed amenities, and residential offering should be assessed against current official project information rather than assumed from a project name or third-party listing.

## Why the links in AI recommendations matter

Appearing in an AI answer does not mean that answer provides a link to the hotel’s own website.

AGR’s [November 2026 AI Hotel Link Study](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-hotel-link-study/) examined **900 answers** from ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, and Gemini. The study used 100 hotel questions across ten destinations, with each question asked three times on each system. Of the **899 answers that named at least one hotel**, **536 (59.6%) contained no hotel or hotel-brand website link anywhere in the answer**. The remaining answer named no hotel from the study’s verified destination lists. The study measured links in saved answers, not clicks, website traffic, or bookings.

That is why link destinations deserve their own check. A property mention, a recommendation, an attached citation, and a visit to the property’s website are different events.

## What to do with the report’s findings

The results help property teams identify distinct issues and assign appropriate next steps.

- **If the property is absent from tested recommendations:** Review which competing properties appeared, whether the questions reflect the property’s positioning, and how its category and distinguishing features are described publicly.
- **If AI describes the property inaccurately:** Compare the statements with current official information and correct discrepancies in sources the property controls.
- **If recommendations link to third parties:** Identify the destinations and assess the direct path available to a traveler or prospective buyer.
- **If the website checks flag technical issues:** Have the appropriate team confirm and prioritize fixes to indexing, structured data, metadata, or other sampled-page findings.

The report gives property teams a concrete starting point for investigating how their property appears in AI-generated answers.
