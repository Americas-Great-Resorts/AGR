I’m Mira Martinez, Vice President of Technology at Americas Great Resorts. My work sits at the intersection of technology, marketing, data, and the hospitality industry.

Over the years, I’ve come to appreciate the people who do more than comment on hospitality trends. They share what they’re learning, challenge conventional thinking, and help the rest of us understand what’s changing and why it matters. This list is my way of recognizing 20 of those voices.

This list began as a post on [my LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mira-martinez). It reflects 20 people whose work I find worth following, whether they’re discussing AI, hotel marketing, distribution, guest experience, or the economics of running a hotel.

These are my personal selections, not a formal ranking. The opinions expressed here are my own and do not necessarily represent those of Americas Great Resorts.

## 20 Hospitality Voices Worth Following

### 1. [Max Starkov](https://www.linkedin.com/in/maxstarkov)

**Hotel distribution and digital marketing strategy**

Max is a hospitality technologist, digital strategist, and former CEO of NextGuest who has taught hospitality technology and digital marketing at New York University’s Tisch Center of Hospitality. His work examines how hotels can manage distribution costs, invest in their own digital capabilities, and compete for direct bookings.

### 2. [Stuart Greif](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stuartwgreif)

**Luxury hospitality, customer experience, and AI strategy**

Stuart is Chief Strategy & Innovation Officer at Forbes Travel Guide, following leadership roles in travel and hospitality at Microsoft and J.D. Power. His work connects emerging technology with the service expectations and operating realities of luxury hospitality.

### 3. [Simone Puorto](https://it.linkedin.com/in/puortosimone)

**AI, distribution, and hospitality transformation**

Through Travel Singularity and his work on emerging trends at Hospitality Net, Simone examines how AI and changing technology infrastructure may reshape hotel discovery and distribution. His writing challenges assumptions about the future of search, booking, and service.

### 4. [Andrew Paul](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andrewpaul1)

**AI visibility and email marketing to reach new customers**

Andrew frequently writes about how hotels can reach travelers who have never stayed with them, focusing on AI visibility, direct demand generation, and permissioned email marketing. His work at Americas Great Resorts looks at how demand can originate before a traveler starts searching for a hotel.

*(Full disclosure: Andrew founded AGR and is my boss. He did not threaten to fire me if I left him off this list. At least not directly.)*

### 5. [Josiah Mackenzie](https://www.linkedin.com/in/josiahmackenzie)

**Hospitality leadership and operator conversations**

Josiah publishes Hospitality Daily, a podcast and newsletter built around conversations with hospitality operators, founders, and industry leaders. His interviews offer a practical window into the decisions and experiments shaping hotel operations and guest experiences.

### 6. [Ira Vouk, MBA](https://www.linkedin.com/in/iravouk)

**AI Hospitality Alliance and practical AI adoption**

Ira founded the AI Hospitality Alliance, an industry initiative focused on collaboration and practical AI adoption. She also teaches hospitality technology at San Diego State University and has written books on hotel technology, distribution, and revenue management.

### 7. [Jan Freitag](https://www.linkedin.com/in/janfreitag)

**Hotel performance analytics and market trends**

Jan is National Director of Hospitality Analytics at CoStar Group and previously worked at STR. He interprets hotel performance data and trends in demand, occupancy, rates, and RevPAR for owners, operators, and investors.

### 8. [Tim Peter](https://www.linkedin.com/in/timpeter)

**Digital marketing and customer acquisition**

Tim founded Tim Peter & Associates and writes about digital strategy, customer acquisition, and the ways large technology platforms influence marketing. His work connects digital decisions to measurable business outcomes for hotels and other travel businesses.

### 9. [Sam Weston](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/samuelweston)

**Hotel marketing, AI, and industry publishing**

Sam is Head of AI & Marketing at 80 DAYS and the founder and editor of Hotel Speak. His work examines how hotel marketing teams can adapt their websites, content, and discovery strategies as generative AI changes travel research.

### 10. [Lynn Patchett](https://gr.linkedin.com/in/lynnpatchett)

**Hotel AI visibility research and digital strategy**

Lynn examines how hotels appear in AI-generated recommendations, with a focus on how consistently properties appear across different recommendation scenarios. His research-oriented approach raises questions about the stability and accuracy of AI hotel recommendations.

### 11. [Larry Mogelonsky, P. Eng.](https://ca.linkedin.com/in/larrymogelonsky)

**Hotel asset management and operations**

Larry is an asset-management and owner-representation consultant at Hotel Mogel. He also writes with Adam Mogelonsky about the connections among hotel operations, guest experience, technology, and business performance.

### 12. [Mark Fancourt](https://www.linkedin.com/in/markfancourt)

**Hotel technology and executive strategy**

As a co-founder and principal at TRAVHOTECH, Mark brings a business-first perspective to hotel technology. His commentary asks whether technology decisions solve hotel operating problems and whether organizational structures help or hinder innovation.

### 13. [Martin Soler](https://fr.linkedin.com/in/martinsolerparis)

**Hotel technology marketing and industry commentary**

Martin has worked on brand positioning and demand generation through Soler & Associates and Pragmatik. He also co-founded 10minutes.news and publishes Tell, where he comments on hotel technology, marketing, and the gap between industry promises and practical results.

### 14. [Stephanie Smith, CHDM](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stephanie-sparks-smith)

**Hotel digital marketing and analytics**

Stephanie leads Cogwheel Marketing, a hospitality-focused digital marketing firm. Her work addresses the measurement, channel strategy, and commercial execution challenges facing hotel marketers, including properties preparing to open.

### 15. [Cindy Estis Green](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cindy-estis-green-4554a1)

**Customer acquisition costs and hotel profitability**

Cindy is CEO and co-founder of Kalibri Labs, which analyzes hotel revenue, distribution, and customer acquisition economics. Her work pushes the conversation beyond gross booking volume toward what hotels retain after the costs of acquiring business.

### 16. [Doug Kennedy](https://www.linkedin.com/in/doug-kennedy-7619b51b)

**Hotel sales, reservations, and guest service**

Doug is president and co-founder of Kennedy Training Network. His training and writing focus on hotel reservations, sales conversations, service communication, and the staff behaviors that shape booking conversion and guest experience.

### 17. [Are Morch](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aremorch)

**Practical AI adoption for independent hotels**

Are writes about the technology choices facing independent hotels, including what genuinely helps operators and what may be marketing hype. His perspective is useful for smaller properties weighing AI adoption against time, staffing, and budget constraints.

### 18. [Frederic Gonzalo](https://ca.linkedin.com/in/fredericgonzalo)

**Travel marketing and social media**

Frederic is a travel marketing consultant and author who has written about social media practices in tourism. His work examines how destinations, hotels, and other travel businesses communicate with potential guests across digital channels.

### 19. [Holly Zoba, CHDM](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hollyzoba)

**Hotel sales training and digital marketing**

Holly leads Scout Simply and co-authored HSMAI’s Certified Hospitality Digital Marketer study guide. Her training connects digital marketing literacy with hotel sales, helping commercial teams understand and use digital tools rather than treat them as someone else’s responsibility.

### 20. [Erik Muñoz](https://uk.linkedin.com/in/erikmunoz)

**Hospitality technology partnerships and distribution**

Erik has worked in commercial and partnership roles across hotel technology companies including SiteMinder, PriceMatch, Lybra, and SuitePad. That experience gives him a close view of the vendor relationships and integrations behind hotel technology.

## Why I Follow Different Perspectives

The people on this list don’t all work on the same problems. Some concentrate on guest experience or hotel operations. Others examine distribution economics, AI, sales, marketing, or market data.

That’s the point. There isn’t one vantage point from which you can understand the whole hotel business.

What I value is the body of work behind each name: the questions they keep asking, the evidence they bring, and whether their thinking holds up over time. A single viral post tells you very little.

## Make the List Useful

Choose five people whose work is closest to a problem you’re trying to understand. Open their profiles, read beyond the most recent post, and look for the ideas they revisit. Follow a few people who disagree with how you currently see the issue.

Who would you add, and what have you learned from their work?
