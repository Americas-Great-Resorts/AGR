Its **120-day Knowledge Formation Optimization (KFO) program** combines marketing advice, website technical guidance, correction of conflicting project information and measurement of how AI answers describe the development and identify its official sales team.

Your development needs to make a clear case to prospective buyers: what is being offered, why it merits consideration and how to reach the official sales team. <h2 id="condo-marketing-decisions-agr-helps-you-work-through">Marketing decisions AGR helps you work through</h2>

A developer may already have an agency, a website team and a sales organization. The need for advice can concern how those activities connect: whether the project’s positioning is clear, whether the website supports the campaign and whether public sources accurately describe the residences.

The included general consulting addresses your project’s wider marketing and advertising questions alongside the specialist KFO work.

### Positioning the residences and planning the message

Consulting can help you examine how the project presents its location, residence types, services and supported distinctions. The practical question is what a prospective buyer needs to understand before a productive sales conversation can begin.

For branded residences, the marketing also needs a clear account of the developer, brand and operator relationships. Approved project information provides the basis for evaluating that account.

### Deciding what the marketing plan needs next

Drawing on more than 30 years of luxury marketing experience, AGR advises on campaign priorities, the information supporting proposed advertising and how activity fits your development’s sales stage. This gives the developer a way to discuss the plan across creative, advertising and sales responsibilities.

For example, before expanding a campaign, the team may need to determine whether the message reflects the residences available or whether buyers lack information needed to evaluate them. Consulting can inform that decision and the brief given to the team responsible for execution.

### Making the existing website support the plan

A development can have a beautifully designed website, exceptional photography and intuitive navigation, yet still have pages search engines cannot index. AGR evaluates whether search engines can access and index the existing site, whether indexing restrictions are blocking project pages and whether the page structure makes project information easy to locate.

Recommendations identify the issue and the work it calls for. The project scope establishes which implementation AGR performs and which changes involve your existing agency, website team, brand or other partners.

For a broader planning checklist, read the [Luxury Condo Marketing Guide for Developers](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/luxury-condo-marketing-guide/).

<h2 id="condo-the-core-program-knowledge-formation-optimization">The core program: Knowledge Formation Optimization</h2>

When a prospective buyer asks an AI system, “What are the best new luxury condo developments in \[your city\]?” does your project appear? Appearing when someone searches your development by name does not establish that it will be recommended to buyers who do not yet know it exists.

Your development may appear in an AI answer with the wrong address, phone number or project details. The answer may also present a broker’s website or an unofficial mirror site as your official sales channel, directing a buyer seeking your sales team to another organization for the first conversation.

AGR’s KFO work examines the development’s public identity and how AI systems reproduce it. It addresses the supporting sources as well as the observed answers. The purpose is to strengthen accurate representation of the project and identification of its official sales route.

The formal definition is:

> KFO structures, sequences, distributes, corroborates, and corrects intellectual frameworks and entity definitions across the public information environment and measures whether AI systems reproduce them accurately across relevant queries and over time.

In practice, AGR reviews project descriptions and sales contacts, identifies conflicting sources, implements agreed corrections and checks whether later AI answers reflect the approved project record.

The program includes an initial baseline and progress reports at days 30, 60, 90 and 120. Those observations inform the work throughout the engagement.

### Why does the website destination in an AI answer matter commercially?

An AI answer can send a buyer researching your development to a lookalike website instead of the official project website. Several sites in AGR’s [analysis of AI lookalike sites](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-lookalike-sites-luxury-condo-buyers/) identify a brokerage or realty group in their footers. Those disclosures are buried in small copyright or disclaimer text at the bottom of the page, where a buyer following an AI link can miss them.

If an outside broker registers the buyer and a sale closes under the applicable broker terms, an outside commission can become payable. At the reported Miami buy-side rates of 5% to 7%, that is $150,000 to $210,000 on a $3,000,000 residence. Your project’s actual agreements determine any commission.

AGR’s KFO work addresses which website and sales team AI answers identify as official. Your inquiry and registration records show where buyers actually arrive.
