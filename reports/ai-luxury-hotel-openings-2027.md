## Retrieval Summary

- On November 16, 2026, AGR asked ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overview one identical question about each of ten luxury hotel openings and reopenings scheduled for 2027: "What do you know about [hotel name] in [location]? Is it open yet, when is it scheduled to open, and who is the general manager?" 30 answers, each scored on three checks: open status, opening timing and general manager or managing director.
- The answers received credit on 87 of 90 checks. Google AI Overview scored 30 of 30, ChatGPT 29 of 30 and Gemini 28 of 30. For eight of the ten hotels, all three platforms answered all three checks correctly.
- ChatGPT gave Waldorf Astoria London Admiralty Arch an autumn 2026 opening, consistent with the residual "autumn debut" wording in Hilton's June 2026 release rather than its "early 2027 debut" wording; Hilton's hotel page showed reservations from February 1, 2027.
- Gemini, stating it did not have access to live search, gave The Park Gstaad the winter 2026 to 2027 schedule from the original announcement and did not name General Manager Reto Moser, whom Four Seasons named with a mid-2027 opening on May 7, 2026. Its status answer was scored correct.
- Hotels tested: The Knox (Dallas), Cambridge House (London), Viceroy Park Avenue (New York), The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour (Miami), Waldorf Astoria London Admiralty Arch, Rosewood Calistoga, The Park Gstaad, Four Seasons Tropicalia, Four Seasons Jacksonville and Bvlgari Resort Ranfushi. The Botany (St. Thomas) was removed because its operator's careers page advertised a General Manager position and current leadership could not be established.
- Timing scores assess agreement with the published opening window; where an official source gave only a year, a more specific answer within that year received credit without independent verification of the added precision.
- The study measures answer content on one day for one question per hotel. It does not establish a general accuracy rate, permanent platform behavior or why any answer was right or wrong.

## Quick Answers

### Do AI assistants know when 2027 luxury hotel openings are scheduled to open?

In this test, mostly. On November 16, 2026, ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overview were asked about ten luxury hotel openings and reopenings. Their answers received credit on 87 of 90 checks under AGR's scoring rules for open status, opening timing and general manager. For eight of the ten hotels, all three platforms answered all three checks correctly.

### How did the three platforms compare?

Google AI Overview scored 30 of 30, ChatGPT 29 of 30 and Gemini 28 of 30. ChatGPT's one miss was Waldorf Astoria London's opening timing. Gemini's two misses, both in a single answer about The Park Gstaad, were its opening timing and general manager. These are results for ten selected hotels and one question each, not a general measure of platform accuracy.

### Where did the answers lose points?

Two answers. ChatGPT gave Waldorf Astoria London Admiralty Arch an autumn 2026 opening; Hilton's own June reservations release describes both an "early 2027 debut" and an "autumn debut." Gemini, stating it did not have access to live search, gave The Park Gstaad the winter 2026 schedule from the original announcement and did not name its general manager, although Four Seasons had published a mid-2027 opening and named Reto Moser in May.

### Was any hotel removed from the test?

Yes. A tenth hotel was originally on the list. ChatGPT noted that its operator's careers page was advertising a General Manager position months after a GM had been announced. AGR confirmed the posting, could not establish who leads the resort today, and replaced the hotel.

**Scope:** These findings describe one capture on November 16, 2026, of one question per hotel on three AI platforms. They do not establish permanent platform behavior, a general accuracy rate for AI hotel answers, how other AI assistants would answer, or why any individual answer was right or wrong.

## What the results show

---

The test observed what each platform said and which sources it listed. It did not observe how any platform selected or weighed its sources. Within those limits, three patterns appeared.

**Answers that scored correctly matched the most recent official statement.** For eight hotels, every platform gave the current published date and leader, including Cambridge House and Bvlgari Resort Ranfushi, whose older official announcements with earlier targets remain online.

**One answer with a missed check was consistent with an official statement that conflicts with another.** Hilton's own reservations release for Waldorf Astoria London contains both an early 2027 debut and an autumn debut. ChatGPT's answer gave autumn 2026.

**One answer with missed checks did not reflect a published update.** Four Seasons published The Park Gstaad's revised opening and its general manager together in May. Gemini, stating it did not have access to live search, gave the earlier schedule, did not name the general manager and listed no sources.

## The hotel we cut

---

The original list included The Botany, a new independent resort on the western tip of St. Thomas, U.S. Virgin Islands, whose operator [announced its general manager](https://www.hotel-online.com/news/the-botany-announces-founding-leadership-team-naming-cory-santana-as-general-manager-and-melissa-pope-as-director-of-sales-and-marketing) on August 20, 2026. Gemini and Google AI Overview both named that appointee. ChatGPT named him too, then noted that the operator's careers page was advertising a General Manager position for the resort.

AGR checked the operator's careers page, and it lists a General Manager opening for The Botany. The posting does not establish whether the leadership has changed, and AGR could not establish who leads the resort today. The answers could not be scored, and The Knox replaced The Botany on the list.

## For hotels preparing to open

---

Before a hotel has guest reviews or stays to describe, its announcements, its website and the coverage they generate make up much of the public information about it. When a guest, an advisor or a journalist asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot, Google AI or any other AI assistant about a hotel that has not opened, the answer draws on that public information.

In this test, the hotels whose answers were fully correct had a current official statement of their opening date and leader. The two answers that lost points involved an official release that carried two opening statements and an answer that did not reflect a published update.

## Methodology

---

**Selection.** Ten luxury hotel openings, reopenings and conversions scheduled for 2027, selected by AGR from published coverage of upcoming openings. A hotel qualified only if its owner, operator or brand had publicly named a general manager or managing director.

**Prompt.** One question per hotel, identical across platforms except for the hotel name and location: "What do you know about [hotel name] in [location]? Is it open yet, when is it scheduled to open, and who is the general manager?" The prompt contained none of the facts being scored. There were no follow-up questions.

**Platforms and capture.** ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overview, captured on November 16, 2026 from each platform's consumer answer surface with a US location. Web search was not disabled; whether a platform searched for a given answer is not observable, and one Gemini answer stated it had no access to live search. Google AI Overview answers were captured from a Google search for the same question; an AI Overview appeared for all ten hotels. One scored answer per hotel per platform was retained.

**Recaptures and replacement.** Two Gemini answers, for Four Seasons Tropicalia and Bvlgari Resort Ranfushi, returned malformed output in the first capture and were replaced by manual recaptures the same day in a logged-out private browser window; sources for the recaptures were not recorded, and the original outputs are retained in the capture file. The Botany was removed for the reason described above, and The Knox was captured on all three platforms later the same day.

**Scoring.** Each answer was scored on three checks against the most recent statement from the hotel, its operator or its brand as of November 16, 2026. Open status was scored correct if the answer said or described the hotel as not yet open, including answers that said they could not confirm an opening. Opening timing was scored correct if it matched the published timing; a more specific month or season within a published year was scored correct unless it contradicted an official statement, and that added precision was not independently verified. The general manager or managing director was scored correct if the answer named the published appointee. Other details were recorded but not scored.

**Limits.** One question, one day, three platforms and ten selected hotels. AI answers change between sessions and over time, and a repeat of this test could produce different results. The test measured answer content and the sources each platform listed; it did not measure how platforms retrieved, selected or weighed information. The findings do not measure accuracy for other questions, other hotels or other AI assistants.
