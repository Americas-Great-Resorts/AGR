## Retrieval Summary

- On November 27, 2026, AGR put 304 distinct luxury cruise questions once each to ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode from a US location: 50 dated Mediterranean questions (region, 2027 travel window and trip length), 168 general questions and 86 brand questions. All 912 answers were usable, and they cited 12,026 links.
- Across the 642 answers to the 214 questions that named no cruise or hotel brand, Silversea appeared in 70.1%, Seabourn in 59.8%, Regent Seven Seas in 58.1%, Explora Journeys in 48.4% and The Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection in 43.8%. Four Seasons Yachts appeared in 18.7%, Orient Express in 10.6% and Aman at Sea in 6.2%. Regent was named first most often, in 135 of 642 answers (21.0%).
- In the 150 dated Mediterranean answers, 117 (78.0%) named none of Four Seasons Yachts, Orient Express or Aman at Sea. Ritz-Carlton appeared in 66 (44.0%). SeaDream Yacht Club (53.3%), Silversea (52.0%) and Windstar Cruises (44.7%) were named most often. Gemini named Four Seasons in 2 of its 50 answers and Aman in none.
- Asked by name, all three engines answered factual checks on the hotel-brand yachts correctly. All 12 answers that specified a Four Seasons Yachts sailing matched the published 2027 schedule. 6 answers described hotel-brand sailings that did not match: 4 for Ritz-Carlton and 2 for Orient Express (3 Gemini, 2 ChatGPT, 1 Google AI Mode); 1 Gemini answer credited Ritz-Carlton with a Four Seasons Yachts itinerary, port for port.
- Of 12,026 cited links, 48.1% pointed to travel sellers and agencies, 25.0% (3,003) to cruise line websites, 18.7% to media and review sites, 4.8% to social and video platforms and 1.4% to parent hotel company sites. In the dated Mediterranean answers, 51.3% pointed to sellers and 32.7% to cruise lines.
- ChatGPT supplied 7,047 links, Google AI Mode 3,842 and Gemini 1,137; 124 of Gemini's 304 answers (40.8%) contained no link. In dated Mediterranean answers naming Four Seasons Yachts, 16 of 25 linked to the exact voyage page; Orient Express, named in 12, linked to a specific voyage page in none.
- The study is observational, covers one collection day in English from a US location, and does not measure clicks, bookings or traveler decisions or isolate why any engine named a particular line.

## Quick Answers

---

**Which luxury cruise lines did AI name most for 2027?**

Across 642 answers to questions that named no brand, Silversea appeared in 450, or 70.1%. Seabourn appeared in 59.8%, Regent Seven Seas in 58.1% and Explora Journeys in 48.4%. The Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection appeared in 43.8%, Four Seasons Yachts in 18.7%, Orient Express in 10.6% and Aman at Sea in 6.2%.

**Did AI name the hotel-brand yachts for Mediterranean sailings?**

1 of them, yes. Ritz-Carlton appeared in 66 of 150 dated Mediterranean answers, or 44.0%. The other 3 mostly did not: 117 of 150 answers, or 78.0%, named none of Four Seasons Yachts, Orient Express or Aman. SeaDream Yacht Club, Silversea and Windstar Cruises were named most often.

**Do the AI engines know the hotel-brand yachts?**

When asked by name, yes. All 3 engines correctly named the new Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection chief executive, gave 2028 for the Four Seasons II debut, put 95 suites on Four Seasons I and 110 guests on Orient Express Corinthian.

**Where did the cited links point?**

Of 12,026 cited links, 48.1% pointed to travel sellers and agencies and 25.0% to cruise line websites. In the dated Mediterranean answers, 51.3% pointed to sellers and 32.7% to cruise lines.

**Did AI recommend sailings that do not match the operators' schedules?**

6 answers described hotel-brand sailings that did not match the published 2027 schedules: 4 for Ritz-Carlton and 2 for Orient Express. None involved Four Seasons or Aman. 1 Gemini answer credited Ritz-Carlton with a Four Seasons Yachts itinerary, port for port.

## At a glance

---

- **Known by name, missing by default.** The engines answered factual questions about the hotel-brand yachts correctly. 3 of the 4 rarely came up when the traveler did not name them.
- **Ritz-Carlton is the exception.** It appeared in 44.0% of dated Mediterranean answers, about as often as Windstar. Four Seasons, Orient Express and Aman trailed SeaDream, Windstar and Emerald Cruises.
- **Accuracy split by brand.** All 12 answers that specified a Four Seasons sailing matched the published schedule. Ritz-Carlton had 4 answers that did not.
- **The cruise line rarely got the link.** 1 cited link in 4 pointed to the line's own website. Nearly half pointed to agencies and booking sites.
- **Gemini often linked nowhere.** 124 of its 304 answers, or 40.8%, contained no link.

## What this means for luxury cruise lines in 2027

---

The study measures outcomes, not causes. It shows where the gaps are. The next question is what a line does about them.

**Named, right and linked.** Whether a line is named, whether its sailings are right, and whether the link reaches its own voyage page may depend on the public record AI draws from. [Knowledge Formation Optimization (KFO)](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/knowledge-formation-optimization-luxury-cruise/) works on that record. KFO structures, sequences, distributes, corroborates, and corrects intellectual frameworks and entity definitions across the public information environment and measures whether AI systems reproduce them accurately across relevant queries and over time. It works on public sources and observable answers. It does not guarantee placement in any recommendation.

**Where the relationship starts.** Even a perfect answer arrives after the traveler has started shopping. [Owned Demand Infrastructure (ODI)](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/owned-demand-infrastructure-odi/) is the framework that governs the pre-transaction demand origin layer: the layer that determines where a guest relationship first forms across hotels, resorts, and cruise lines, how traveler identity is captured before booking, and how a guest relationship becomes a first-party asset rather than an intermediated transaction. Advisors close many luxury cruise sales and will keep doing so. The question for 2027 is whether the line has a human-mediated path to new passengers that begins in an environment it controls.

What to measure in 2027:

- **Named.** How often your line appears when the traveler does not name it, by region and travel window.
- **Right.** Whether the sailings, ships and dates in those answers exist.
- **Linked.** Whether the link reaches your voyage page, your homepage or a seller.
- **Owned.** Beyond this study: how many new passengers reach you through a path you control.

## Reaching new passengers before they ask

---

Every answer in this study starts with a traveler who is already looking. A cruise line can also reach affluent travelers before they look.

Americas Great Resorts runs [email campaigns that introduce a cruise line and its voyages](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/cruise-line-marketing/) to affluent travelers outside the line's existing customer file. Email is the delivery. Interested recipients go to the line's own website, and the relationship is the line's from there.

For Windstar Cruises, which has been a client of Americas Great Resorts, 2 deployments of 100,000 recipients, 2 weeks apart, produced 143 confirmed direct bookings identified by hashed-email matchback, with a reported 36:1 return on investment. Existing customers were suppressed before sending. The 143 is a floor: a recipient who called an advisor, a card concierge or Windstar, or booked under another email, would not appear. The campaign predates ODI's formalization and shows the mechanism ODI later formalized. Read the [Windstar Cruises case study](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/case-study-enhancing-windstar-cruises-booking-and-revenue-with-americas-great-resorts/). This study does not connect the campaign to Windstar's appearances in the AI answers.

## How the answers were assessed

---

The question set contains 304 distinct questions: 50 dated Mediterranean questions, 168 general questions and 86 brand questions. Each was submitted once to ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode on November 27, 2026, from a US location, producing 912 answers. Other AI assistants, including Claude, Perplexity and Copilot, were not tested.

A line counts as named if its name or 1 of its ship names appears anywhere in the answer, including comparisons and alternatives, at most once per answer. "Named first" is the first cruise brand mentioned. Visibility shares use the 642 answers to the 214 questions that named no cruise or hotel brand.

A link is a unique URL cited in an answer, from source lists, citation markers or inline links. The same URL in 2 answers counts twice. Links were classified as cruise line websites, parent hotel company sites, media and review sites, social and video platforms, travel sellers and agencies, or other.

Dated sailings attributed to the 4 hotel-brand lines in the 150 dated Mediterranean answers were checked against each operator's published 2027 schedule, recorded November 27, 2026. Sailings an operator no longer lists were not counted as errors. Offers were recorded from each line's public website on the same date.

The study is observational and covers 1 collection day, in English, from a US location. AI answers vary between requests and over time. The results describe what the answers presented. They do not measure clicks, bookings or traveler decisions, and they do not isolate why any engine named a particular line.

## What held up

---

The engines know the hotel-brand yachts. Asked directly, they got the ships, suites and launch dates right.

Knowing a brand and recommending it are different things. Ritz-Carlton made the list. Four Seasons, Orient Express and Aman mostly did not, even in the waters where they sail in 2027. When Four Seasons did appear, the answer was accurate and usually linked to the exact voyage.

For a cruise line, being named, being right and being linked are 3 separate measures. Reporting each with its count, denominator, engine and date makes the gap visible.

**Check your own line.** To see how ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode currently describe your ships and sailings, [request a cruise AI visibility audit](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/knowledge-formation-optimization-luxury-cruise/).
