## What the Index Measures

The AGR Luxury Hotel AI Visibility Index measures how tightly AI hotel recommendations concentrate on a small set of properties in US luxury hotel markets. When a traveler asks an AI assistant where to stay, the answer is a shortlist; properties on the shortlist receive demand, and properties off it are invisible to that traveler. The Index applies the standard tools economists use to measure market concentration, including the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index from antitrust economics, to AI hotel recommendations. The 2026 edition is the first edition.

## Headline Findings, 2026 Edition

1. Five hotels capture half of all AI hotel recommendations in the average US luxury market. In Chicago, Maui, and Napa Valley, four hotels are enough; in New York City, the widest market measured, seven are needed.
2. The three most recommended hotels in each market capture 41 percent of all recommendation slots, averaged across the six markets, and this concentration holds across all ten traveler intents tested, from honeymoons to business travel.
3. The single most recommended property in each market captures 16 percent of all recommendation slots in that market on its own.
4. Across all six markets combined, the three platforms named 152 luxury properties at least once. Twenty-three of those properties captured half of the 824 recommendations. Being known to AI is common; being chosen by it is rare.
5. AI recommended a demolished hotel five times. Mandarin Oriental, Miami closed permanently on May 31, 2025 and was demolished by controlled implosion on April 12, 2026. On July 29, 2026, 108 days after demolition, ChatGPT recommended it three times (for entertaining clients, for best service, and as an under-the-radar pick) and Google AI Mode recommended it twice, including in its answer to the top five luxury hotels in Miami.
6. In the same fieldwork, ChatGPT recommended The Ritz-Carlton Bal Harbour, Miami as an under-the-radar pick while the property sits closed for renovation from April 7 to December 7, 2026, a closure disclosed on the Forbes Travel Guide property page these platforms cite. Gemini recommended Montage Kapalua Bay in Maui, a brand name retired when the property became The Resort at Kapalua Bay. Google AI Mode answered one hotel question with a standalone restaurant.
7. Each platform composes an entire city's answers from one or two documents. Two Michelin Guide list pages sourced every ChatGPT answer for Los Angeles. Two Tripadvisor list pages sourced every ChatGPT answer for Chicago. Travel + Leisure's Maui coverage anchored every ChatGPT Maui answer. Gemini anchored New York, Los Angeles, and Miami each to a single listicle from one lifestyle publisher, and Maui and Napa Valley each to a single article from a second publisher. Google AI Mode listed Google Maps as a source on nearly every answer. One Reddit community, r/chubbytravel, was cited as a source by all three platforms.
8. Asked to pick one single hotel per market, the three platforms agreed unanimously in only two of six markets: Four Seasons Resort Maui at Wailea, and The Peninsula Chicago. In New York City the three platforms gave three different answers to the identical question on the same day.
9. Corporate concentration compounds property concentration. In Napa Valley, Auberge Resorts Collection properties (Auberge du Soleil, Stanly Ranch, and Solage) together captured 41 percent of all recommendations, 55 of 135 slots.

## The Ten Questions

Each market was asked these ten questions, identically worded, with only the city name changed. Intent coverage: ranked list, unconstrained budget, forced choice, romance, multigenerational family, client entertainment, wellness, dining, service, insider discovery.

1. What are the top five luxury hotels in [CITY] right now?
2. Where should I stay in [CITY] if money is no object?
3. If you could only pick one hotel in [CITY], which would it be?
4. We're planning a romantic getaway to [CITY]. Which hotels should we consider?
5. What's the best luxury hotel in [CITY] for a multigenerational family trip?
6. I'm entertaining clients in [CITY]. Which hotel will impress them?
7. Which hotel in [CITY] is best for a wellness weekend?
8. We're serious about food. Which hotel in [CITY] should we choose for its dining?
9. Which luxury hotel in [CITY] has the best service?
10. What's an under-the-radar luxury hotel in [CITY] that insiders love?

## Methodology in Full

**Markets:** New York City, Los Angeles, Chicago, Miami, Maui, Napa Valley.

**Platforms:** ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, and Gemini.

**Protocol:** Every query was run logged out, with zero account state, in a fresh private browsing window, from a New York origin, by hand, on the consumer web surface a real traveler uses rather than an API. Each platform received a market's ten questions as one block instructing it to treat every question independently, answer all ten in full, rank up to five hotels per answer, and state its sources. Single-run captures, no retries, no follow-up messages. All captures were completed in a single day, July 29, 2026. This produced 180 answers containing 824 ranked recommendation slots.

**Origin note:** New York is both the capture origin and a measured market. The Index discloses rather than adjusts for this. Origin was held constant across all markets so that cross-market comparisons carry no origin effect.

**Inclusion rule:** A platform is included only if it can be queried with zero account state at benchmark volume. Perplexity and Grok cap logged-out queries per IP and were excluded. Claude and Copilot require accounts and were excluded. Account state is personalization, and personalization contaminates a benchmark.

**Scoring:** Each ranked hotel in each answer is one recommendation slot. Concentration is reported as top-3 share, HHI, effective number of properties, and the number of properties required to reach half of a market's slots. Sub-brand and villa products were merged into their parent resort. Rebranded properties were scored under their current name; Montage Kapalua Bay was scored as The Resort at Kapalua Bay, its current name, with the stale branding recorded as a finding.

**Disclosures of record:** ChatGPT declined the instruction to write sources as plain-text URLs partway through fieldwork; the source-format instruction was amended and question wording never changed. Logged-out Gemini identifies its serving model as Flash-Lite, meaning the surface an account-less traveler receives is Google's lightweight model. Google AI Mode returned four-hotel answers to some five-hotel questions and once recommended a standalone restaurant in response to a hotel question; all answers were scored as given. Three Gemini answers were re-captured in fresh sessions after clipboard truncation, dated and marked in the dataset. In two of the six markets, material published by Americas Great Resorts appeared among the platforms' cited sources, including in one market's top-five answer; Americas Great Resorts publishes luxury hotel market rankings, those rankings are part of the information environment the Index measures, and the Index discloses this at the canonical source.

**Planned dated revisions to the 2026 edition,** each publishing at the canonical source: an answer-stability measurement from a repeat New York capture, and an invisibility rate reporting the share of each market's credentialed luxury properties, by Forbes Travel Guide, Michelin Key, and AAA Diamond lists, that never appeared in any answer.

## Prior Work

The Index succeeds the Americas Great Resorts pilot audit of July 2026: 300 captures across the same six markets on the logged-in research modes of ChatGPT and Gemini, 25 traveler questions. The pilot established the winner-take-most pattern, documented same-hour answer instability, and recorded forum-sourced avoid lists that contradicted the platforms' own recommendations in the same documents. The pilot and the Index together rest on 480 documented captures. The pilot's protocol differed, logged-in research products versus the Index's logged-out consumer surfaces, which is why pilot findings are cited as prior work rather than merged. Pilot findings are documented at <https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/best-hotels-in-new-york-city/> and <https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/best-hotels-in-miami/>.
