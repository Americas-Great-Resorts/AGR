## Published Research Library

<!-- agr:published-library-start -->

In AGR’s July 2026 Luxury Hotel AI Visibility Index, an average of five hotels captured half of the ranked recommendations within the six US luxury markets studied. That concentration makes entry into the traveler’s shortlist a commercial question for hotel owners and marketing teams. [Read the Index (PDF)](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-visibility-index-2026.pdf).

This library brings together AGR’s property-level research on luxury hotels, resorts and luxury residential condo developments. The reports show which properties appear in the questions tested, how accurately they are described, and whether links lead to the property, a booking platform, a broker, a [lookalike website](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/ai-lookalike-sites-luxury-condo-buyers/) or another destination.

For hotel and residential condo sales teams, those are distinct questions: Are we recommended when buyers do not name us? Is our offering represented correctly? Where does the answer direct the next inquiry?

The collection includes strong visibility as well as omissions. Being recognized by name is different from being recommended, and a positive recommendation can still contain inaccurate details or an unclear inquiry route.

## What these reports help owners and marketing teams examine

- **Discovery:** Does the property appear when a traveler or prospective buyer searches without naming it?
- **Representation:** Do the answers preserve important distinctions about location, facilities, services, development status and residential collections?
- **Inquiry destinations:** Do captured links lead to the property website, a booking platform, a broker or another source?
- **Public-source consistency:** Where examined, do the property’s own pages and other published descriptions agree?

Inclusion in this library does not establish a client relationship or property endorsement.

## Luxury hotel and resort visibility reports

### Eau Palm Beach Resort & Spa

**Market:** Manalapan, Florida | **Report date:** October 19, 2026

The resort appears in every completed local recommendation answer in this sample. The sampled answers nevertheless contain room-view claims, dining descriptions and alternate-domain links that require separate review.

[Read the Eau Palm Beach Resort & Spa report (PDF)](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/AGR-Eau-Palm-Beach-Resort-Spa-Visibility-Report.pdf)

### Hammock Beach Golf Resort & Spa

**Market:** Palm Coast, Florida | **Report date:** October 19, 2026

Strong inclusion across the sampled local questions provides a positive discovery example. Alongside those recommendations, the findings cover AI commentary about accommodation condition and inconsistencies in published dining counts, product pricing and page descriptions.

[Read the Hammock Beach report (PDF)](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/AGR-Hammock-Beach-Golf-Resort-Spa-Visibility-Report.pdf)

### InterContinental Miami

**Market:** Miami, Florida | **Report date:** October 13, 2026

All three engines recognize the hotel when named directly, but omit it from the sampled local recommendation answers. Luxury, downtown-attraction, dining and wellness questions expose the difference between recognition and discovery; the website review separately identifies conflicting bayfront and oceanfront descriptions.

[Read the InterContinental Miami report (PDF)](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/AGR-InterContinental-Miami-Visibility-Report.pdf)

### LondonHouse Chicago

**Market:** Chicago, Illinois | **Report date:** October 18, 2026

One AI answer confuses two dining venues and their floors, while recommendations vary across engines. The website review also found inconsistent hotel addresses, Book Now links carrying 2022 dates, and core pages that point search engines to a different domain.

[Read the LondonHouse Chicago report (PDF)](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/AGR-LondonHouse-Chicago-Visibility-Report.pdf)

### Salamander Middleburg

**Market:** Middleburg, Virginia | **Report date:** October 13, 2026

The resort is recommended in every completed local answer in the sample. Remaining accuracy issues include an off-site activity described as on-property, inconsistent dining-venue names and an event page that places a venue in another state.

[Read the Salamander Middleburg report (PDF)](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/AGR-Salamander-Middleburg-Visibility-Report.pdf)

### Sensira Resort & Spa

**Market:** Puerto Morelos, Mexico | **Report date:** October 15, 2026

Direct recognition does not carry into the sampled local recommendations. The findings also include conflicting restaurant-availability descriptions and a similarly branded booking domain whose operator and commercial relationship remain unverified.

[Read the Sensira Resort & Spa report (PDF)](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/AGR-Sensira-Resort-Spa-Visibility-Report.pdf)

### Shutters on the Beach

**Market:** Santa Monica, California | **Report date:** October 19, 2026

Luxury, beachfront and romantic-stay recommendations are strong, while dining and wellness inclusion varies across engines. One captured dining-reservation link points to a generic platform homepage, while the inspected official dining page refers guests to a property-specific route on another platform.

[Read the Shutters on the Beach report (PDF)](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/AGR-Shutters-on-the-Beach-Visibility-Report.pdf)

### The Edgewater Hotel

**Market:** Seattle, Washington | **Report date:** October 13, 2026

Luxury, attraction-access and romantic-stay recommendations vary across engines. One AI description turns historic window fishing into a current guest service, without confirmation in the inspected official material. Dining recommendations also illustrate the difference between links to general hotel information and a specific restaurant inquiry destination.

[Read The Edgewater Hotel report (PDF)](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/AGR-The-Edgewater-Hotel-Visibility-Report.pdf)

### The Oxford Hotel

**Market:** Denver, Colorado | **Report date:** October 10, 2026

Gemini displays an Oxford spa-and-gym card without recommending the hotel in its wellness narrative, while Google AI Mode recognizes the offering. The findings distinguish a visible card from an actual recommendation and examine how dining venues and published contact numbers are associated with the hotel.

[Read The Oxford Hotel report (PDF)](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/AGR-The-Oxford-Hotel-Visibility-Report.pdf)
