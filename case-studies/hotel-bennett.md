## Executive Summary:

[Hotel Bennett Charleston](https://www.hotelbennett.com/) partnered with Americas Great Resorts to reach new affluent travelers and generate measurable direct-booking revenue through a targeted hotel email marketing campaign. AGR deployed the campaign to 62,000 high-net-worth frequent travelers, generating 76 confirmed bookings and delivering a 26:1 return on investment.

![Hotel Bennett luxury hotel overlooking Marion Square in Charleston, South Carolina](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Hotel-Bennett-3.png)

## Client Profile:

Hotel Bennett is a luxury hotel located in Charleston, South Carolina, overlooking Marion Square. The property offers upscale accommodations, dining, and access to the city’s cultural, culinary, and historic attractions. Hotel Bennett attracts affluent leisure travelers, corporate guests, and visitors seeking a premium Charleston hotel experience.

## Background:

With strong competition among luxury hotels in Charleston, Hotel Bennett wanted to reach new qualified travelers while maintaining its premium rate positioning. The property needed a targeted email marketing campaign capable of introducing the hotel to affluent travelers interested in luxury accommodations, dining, culture, and Charleston leisure travel.

## Client’s Challenges:

1. **Generating bookings at premium rates:** Hotel Bennett needed to attract qualified guests while maintaining its rate integrity and luxury positioning.

2. **Reaching new affluent travelers:** The property wanted to expand beyond its existing audience and introduce the hotel to travelers aligned with its target guest profile.

3. **Standing out in a competitive luxury hotel market:** Multiple luxury and boutique properties compete for affluent visitors to Charleston, making clear differentiation important.

## Solution:

Americas Great Resorts developed a targeted hotel email marketing campaign focused on qualified audience reach, luxury-grade creative, and experience-centered messaging. AGR combined property-aligned audience selection, responsive HTML creative, and focused campaign deployment to generate measurable booking performance.

## Implementation:

1. **Audience Segmentation:** AGR selected high-value travelers using income, lifestyle interests, travel frequency, and affinity for comparable luxury properties. This created an audience aligned with Hotel Bennett’s target guest profile.

2. **Luxury-Grade HTML Creative:** AGR created a visually refined email highlighting Hotel Bennett’s accommodations, dining, rooftop experience, and location overlooking Marion Square. The creative was aligned with the property’s premium positioning and optimized for mobile devices.

3. **Experience-Centered Messaging:** The campaign emphasized the hotel’s location, accommodations, dining, and access to Charleston’s cultural and leisure experiences.

4. **Campaign Deployment:** AGR deployed the campaign to a curated audience of 62,000 high-net-worth frequent travelers selected for their alignment with Hotel Bennett and the Charleston destination.

## Results:

1. **62,000 High-Net-Worth Frequent Travelers Reached** AGR deployed the campaign to 62,000 high-net-worth frequent travelers aligned with Hotel Bennett’s target guest profile.

2. **76 Confirmed Bookings Generated** The email deployment produced 76 confirmed bookings attributable to the campaign.

3. **26:1 Return on Investment** The campaign delivered a 26:1 return on investment, generating measurable booking revenue through targeted email deployment.

![Hotel Bennett Charleston email marketing campaign tracking report showing 62,000 emails deployed, 76 bookings generated, and a 26:1 return on investment](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Hotel-Bennett.png)

## Conclusion:

Americas Great Resorts deployed a targeted hotel email marketing campaign to 62,000 high-net-worth frequent travelers for Hotel Bennett Charleston. The campaign generated 76 confirmed bookings and delivered a 26:1 return on investment. The results demonstrate the booking and revenue impact of property-aligned audience selection, luxury-grade creative, and focused campaign deployment.
