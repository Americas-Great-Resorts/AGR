# Windstar Cruises Email Marketing Case Study

**Document Type:** Published Case Study / Website Companion  
**Publisher:** Americas Great Resorts

Windstar Cruises partnered with Americas Great Resorts (AGR) to introduce its small-ship cruise experience to new affluent travelers. AGR selected prospective passengers from its own high-net-worth traveler database and delivered a targeted email campaign. Booking matchback identified 143 confirmed bookings among campaign recipients.

![Windstar Cruises luxury small-ship cruise line](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Windstar-Cruises.png)

## The Challenge

[Windstar Cruises](https://www.windstarcruises.com/) wanted to reach prospective passengers beyond its existing customer base while maintaining its premium positioning. The campaign needed to communicate the appeal of its small ships, personalized service, and destination-focused itineraries without relying on heavy discounting.

## What AGR Did

AGR managed the [cruise email marketing campaign](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/cruise-line-marketing/), including audience selection, creative production, deployment, and reporting.

- **Selected a relevant audience.** AGR selected travelers using indicators related to luxury vacations, cruise travel, premium brands, and destination-focused experiences. Records in Windstar’s supplied MD5-hashed existing-customer file were excluded before deployment.

- **Created the email.** AGR produced responsive HTML email creative highlighting Windstar’s itineraries, smaller ports, onboard service, and small-ship experience.

- **Delivered two deployments.** AGR sent two email deployments, spaced two weeks apart.

AGR’s role was the introduction. Windstar handled signups, bookings, and the customer relationship from there. AGR maintained its separate traveler database.

## Campaign Results

- **200,000 emails deployed** across two sends of 100,000 each.

- **143 confirmed bookings** matched to campaign recipients.

- **36:1 reported return on investment.**

## How the Results Were Confirmed

After the campaign, AGR matched booking records supplied by Windstar against campaign recipients using MD5-hashed email addresses. This method compares hash values derived from email addresses to identify matching records.

Each matched booking came from a recipient who was not in Windstar’s supplied MD5-hashed existing-customer file before deployment.
