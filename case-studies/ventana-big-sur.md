## Published Case Study

<!-- agr:published-article-start -->

## Executive Summary:

Ventana Big Sur is one of California’s most recognized luxury coastal resorts, but even established properties need effective ways to reach new direct-booking guests. Americas Great Resorts executed a targeted luxury hotel email marketing campaign that reached 44,000 qualified travel prospects and generated 58 new bookings at an average daily rate above $1,000. The campaign produced measurable booking and revenue results while introducing the resort to affluent travelers interested in luxury California getaways.

![Ventana Big Sur Resort, a luxury coastal property in California](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Ventana-Big-Sur-1.jpg)

## Client Profile:

Ventana Big Sur is a luxury resort located along California’s central coast, offering all-inclusive accommodations, elevated dining, wellness programming, and immersive nature experiences overlooking the Pacific. The resort attracts affluent travelers seeking privacy, natural beauty, and a high-touch luxury experience.

## Background:

[Ventana Big Sur](https://www.ventanabigsur.com/) wanted to increase bookings and revenue while maintaining its position as a premium luxury destination. Despite its distinctive setting and high average daily rate, the resort competes with other upscale California properties for affluent leisure travelers. Ventana Big Sur needed a way to reach more qualified guests directly, communicate the value of its all-inclusive experience, and convert interest into high-value stays without weakening rate integrity.

## Client’s Challenges:

1. **Standing out in a competitive luxury market:** Multiple upscale resorts and boutique properties compete for affluent travelers in Big Sur and along the California coast, making clear differentiation important.

2. **Generating bookings at a premium ADR:** With rates exceeding $1,000 per night, Ventana Big Sur needed to generate qualified demand while protecting its average daily rate and luxury positioning.

3. **Expanding the direct-booking audience:** The resort wanted to reach new travelers beyond its existing guest base and generate bookings without relying exclusively on third-party channels.

## Solution:

Americas Great Resorts developed a tailored luxury hotel email marketing campaign for Ventana Big Sur focused on direct bookings and measurable revenue generation. The campaign combined property-aligned audience targeting, premium creative, and offer messaging built around Ventana Big Sur’s all-inclusive positioning, natural setting, and high-value guest experience.

## Implementation:

1. **Strategic Audience Segmentation:** Americas Great Resorts identified and segmented travelers whose interests aligned with the property, including affluent leisure travelers, luxury travelers, wellness enthusiasts, and travelers interested in the Big Sur region. Geographic and demographic indicators were used to create a property-specific targeting model.

2. **Luxury-Grade Email Creative and Messaging:** AGR created visually rich email creative showcasing Ventana Big Sur’s all-inclusive experience, elevated dining, wellness offerings, and coastal setting. The messaging emphasized the distinctive experience and value of a stay at the resort.

3. **Curated Audience and Campaign Deployment:** The campaign was deployed to a curated audience of **44,000 qualified travel prospects** selected for their alignment with luxury California travel and the Ventana Big Sur guest profile.

4. **Performance Measurement:** AGR measured campaign engagement, attributable bookings, and resulting booking performance to evaluate the effectiveness of the deployment.

## Results:

1. **44,000 Qualified Travel Prospects Reached** AGR deployed the campaign to 44,000 travel prospects whose interests and characteristics aligned with Ventana Big Sur’s target guest profile.

2. **58 New Bookings Generated** The targeted hotel email marketing campaign produced 58 new bookings attributable to the deployment.

3. **Average Daily Rate Above $1,000** The generated bookings carried an average daily rate exceeding $1,000 per night, supporting the resort’s premium rate positioning.

4. **Measurable Booking and Revenue Impact** The campaign converted a targeted email audience into high-value stays and measurable direct-booking revenue.

## Conclusion:

Americas Great Resorts deployed a targeted luxury hotel email marketing campaign to 44,000 qualified travel prospects for Ventana Big Sur. The campaign generated 58 new bookings at an average daily rate above $1,000, producing measurable booking and revenue impact while maintaining the property’s premium positioning.

The results demonstrate how property-specific audience targeting, luxury-grade creative, and focused campaign deployment can generate high-value bookings for an established luxury resort.
