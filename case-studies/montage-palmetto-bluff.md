## Executive Summary:

[Montage Palmetto Bluff](https://www.montage.com/palmettobluff/) partnered with Americas Great Resorts to expand its direct-booking audience and reach new affluent travelers through a targeted hotel email marketing campaign. AGR deployed the campaign to 65,000 high-net-worth frequent travelers, generating 91 confirmed bookings and delivering a 27:1 return on investment.

![Montage Palmetto Bluff luxury resort in South Carolina’s Lowcountry](https://www.americasgreatresorts.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Montage-Palmetto-Bluff-2.jpg)

## Client Profile

Montage Palmetto Bluff is a luxury resort located in South Carolina’s Lowcountry, known for its waterfront setting, upscale accommodations, private residences, spa, and outdoor experiences. The resort attracts affluent travelers seeking privacy, refined hospitality, and access to nature-focused recreation.

## Background

With strong competition among luxury resorts across the Southeast, Montage Palmetto Bluff wanted to reach new qualified travelers while maintaining its premium rate positioning. The resort needed a targeted email marketing campaign capable of introducing the property to affluent travelers interested in coastal escapes, wellness experiences, family travel, and luxury accommodations.

## Client’s Challenges:

**Competing in a crowded luxury market:**  
Numerous high-end properties compete for the same affluent traveler audience, making clear differentiation important.

**Generating bookings at premium rates:**  
The resort needed to attract qualified guests without relying on broad discounting.

**Reaching new affluent travelers:**  
Montage Palmetto Bluff wanted to expand beyond its existing guest base and introduce the property to new travelers aligned with its luxury positioning.

## Solution:

Americas Great Resorts developed a tailored hotel email marketing campaign focused on qualified audience reach, premium creative, and experience-centered messaging. AGR combined audience segmentation, responsive HTML creative, and strategically timed deployment to generate measurable booking performance.

## Implementation:

1. **Audience Segmentation:** AGR selected high-value travelers using income, lifestyle interests, travel behavior, and affinity for comparable luxury properties. This created an audience aligned with Montage Palmetto Bluff’s target guest profile.

2. **Luxury-Grade Email Creative:** AGR designed a visually refined email showcasing the resort’s waterfront setting, accommodations, amenities, and Lowcountry experience. The creative was aligned with the property’s premium positioning and optimized for mobile devices.

3. **Experience-Centered Messaging:** The campaign highlighted the resort’s nature, wellness, culinary, recreational, and private-accommodation experiences.

4. **Campaign Deployment:** AGR deployed the campaign to a curated audience of 65,000 high-net-worth frequent travelers selected for their alignment with the property and destination.

## Results:

**65,000 High-Net-Worth Frequent Travelers Reached**  
AGR deployed the campaign to a curated audience of 65,000 high-net-worth frequent travelers aligned with Montage Palmetto Bluff’s ideal guest profile.

**91 Confirmed Bookings Generated**  
The email deployment produced 91 confirmed bookings attributable to the campaign.

**27:1 Return on Investment**  
The campaign delivered a 27:1 return on investment, generating measurable booking revenue through targeted email deployment.

## Conclusion:

Americas Great Resorts deployed a targeted email campaign to 65,000 high-net-worth frequent travelers for Montage Palmetto Bluff. The campaign generated 91 confirmed bookings and delivered a 27:1 return on investment. The results demonstrate the booking and revenue impact of property-aligned audience selection, luxury-grade creative, and focused campaign deployment.
